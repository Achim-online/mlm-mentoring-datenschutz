What data is processed?

The app stores the texts you enter (conversation notes, reflections, questions) exclusively locally on your device. The data is accessible only to you, is not transmitted to us, not synchronized to any cloud, and not shared with third parties.

AI-assisted processing

To generate mentor responses, your text input is transmitted via a secured backend to an external language model service. Processing takes place solely for the purpose of providing the app's functionality. Your data is not stored or shared beyond this purpose.

Your explicit consent is obtained before first use of this feature. You may withdraw consent at any time under Settings → Privacy → Revoke AI Consent.

Automated content

Responses are generated automatically by a language model. They do not constitute professional advice and are labelled accordingly.

Anonymous device identifier

On first use, a random, anonymous device identifier (UUID) is generated and stored in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain or Android Encrypted Shared Preferences). It is transmitted with every API request so the backend can maintain a monthly prompt quota per device. It contains no personal data and is not used for profiling.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed through your App Store or Google Play account. The payment service provider (Apple or Google) receives the data necessary for processing. We only receive an anonymous receipt, which our backend sends to Apple or Google for validation.

Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, and delete your data. Since all text entries are stored exclusively locally on your device, you have full control at all times. Uninstalling the app fully removes your data.
